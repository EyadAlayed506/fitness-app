# Using the pilot app (for testers)

The pilot runs as a **web app**: nothing to install from a store. You'll get, privately from Alzoman,
the **app address** (`https://…`) and **your own email and password**. Don't share your login; your
workouts, food and sleep are only visible to your account.

## Open it

1. Open the address on your phone in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone). A computer browser works too.
2. Log in with your email and password.
3. Optional, so it opens like an app:
   - Android Chrome: menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.
   - iPhone Safari: Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.

## What's different from a phone app

- **Sleep and steps from Health Connect / Apple Health can't be read by a web app.** Enter sleep by hand,
  or connect WHOOP if you use one.
- Machine photos: tapping the camera opens your phone's photo picker; choose **Camera** there to take a new one.
- The rest timer beeps instead of sending a notification: keep the app open during rest.
- If you clear your browser data you'll be logged out (your records stay on the server).

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Wrong email or password" | Check for typos and capital letters; ask Alzoman to reset it |
| "Your session expired. Please log in again." | Log in again. If it keeps happening, tell Alzoman |
| "This account no longer has access to the pilot" | Your access was switched off; ask Alzoman |
| "Can't reach the server at …" or the page won't load | The server PC may be off; try again later and tell the team |
| An old version after an update | Reload the page |

When reporting a problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened, the time, your phone and
browser, and a screenshot. Don't send screenshots that show other people's data, or your password.
