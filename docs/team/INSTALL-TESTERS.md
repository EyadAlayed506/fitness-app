# Installing the pilot app (for testers)

You'll get, privately from Alzoman: a **download link** for the Android app and **your own email and
password**. Don't share your login; your workouts, food and sleep are only visible to your account.

## Android

1. Open the download link on your phone and download the `.apk`.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app (Settings shows the
   switch). This is needed because the app isn't from the Play Store.
3. Open **Fitness App**, log in with your email and password.
4. For sleep: allow Health Connect access when the app asks (Sleep screen, or the Health Connect card on Home). You can change this later
   in the Health Connect app.

## iPhone

Not available yet — the team has no way to sign iPhone builds at the moment. You'll be told when it is.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Wrong email or password" | Check for typos and capital letters; ask Alzoman to reset it |
| "Your session expired. Please log in again." | Log in again. If it keeps happening, tell Alzoman |
| "This account no longer has access to the pilot" | Your access was switched off; ask Alzoman |
| "Can't reach the server at …" | The server PC may be off; try again later and tell the team |

When reporting a problem: what you did, what you expected, what happened, the time, and a screenshot.
Don't send screenshots that show other people's data, or your password.
